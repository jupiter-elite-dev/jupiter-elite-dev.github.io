We do not require you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information to play Tile Tap: Triple Match Puzzle. However, the App may automatically collect certain non-personal information through third-party services to ensure optimal gameplay and improve user experience:

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or serve advertisements. These services operate under their respective privacy policies:

The non-personal information collected is used solely to:

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete such data.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with device and analytical information. While no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, we rely on industry-standard solutions from third-party partners like Google to safeguard data.
